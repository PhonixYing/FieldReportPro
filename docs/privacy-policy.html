Field Report Pro

Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 8, 2026

Field Report Pro is a paid iOS app for creating field inspection and photo PDF reports.

  • No account is required.
  • Report data and attached photos are stored locally on your device.
  • The app does not sell personal data.
  • Data leaves your device only when you choose to export or share a report.

Information We Access

Field Report Pro may request access to the camera and photo library so you can capture or import jobsite photos into your reports.

How Data Is Stored

Report information such as client name, address, notes, issue details, and photos is stored locally using Apple system storage on your device. Field Report Pro does not automatically sync this content to a developer-operated backend service.

Sharing and Export

When you export a PDF or share content using the iOS share sheet, the destination is chosen by you. Once a file is sent to Mail, Files, AirDrop, Messages, or another app or service, that destination’s own privacy practices apply.

Analytics and Tracking

Field Report Pro does not include third-party advertising SDKs and does not use cross-app tracking for advertising purposes.

Data Retention

Because report data is stored locally, you control retention by editing or deleting reports from the app and by managing the app’s data on your device.
